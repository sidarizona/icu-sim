ICU CRITICAL CARE SIMULATOR
HOW TO PLAY Goal: Stabilise the patient within 5 minutes by meeting all targets:
  • MAP ≥ 65 mmHg
  • RASS 0 to −2 (calm, not deeply sedated)
  • BPS ≤ 5 (pain controlled)
  • CAM-ICU negative (no delirium)
  • Lactate < 2.0 mmol/L

Pumps (left panel):
  • Select a drug from the dropdown on each pump
  • Press GO to start an infusion
  • Use + / − to titrate the dose up or down
  • Press STOP to stop an infusion
